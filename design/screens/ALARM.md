# Screen Specification — Alarm

## Goal and primary action
Create, understand and manage alarms; create or toggle an alarm.

## Hierarchy
Next enabled alarm/status; alarm list with time/label/recurrence; create alarm; optional QR/barcode mission; advanced sound, fade, backup and appearance settings.

## Interaction
Enabled/disabled state unmistakable. Recurrence in plain language. Explain exact-alarm/notification permissions in context. Explain fallback behavior and avoid unsupported guarantees. Test scanner failure and camera denial; provide accessible alternative where feasible.

## Acceptance
Basic alarm creation/editing does not require advanced configuration. Next trigger and recurrence are clear. Permission states are honest. Test on physical devices and relevant Android versions.
