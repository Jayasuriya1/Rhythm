# Screen Specification — Routine

## Goal and primary action
Record or review recurring daily anchors quickly and without judgment.

## Content
Five current product anchors: out of bed, first social contact, main activity, dinner and getting into bed. Show target/actual times, plain-language delta, optional reminders and quiet hours. Show a consistency summary only when data coverage is sufficient.

## Rules
Logging is quick; entries can be corrected. Explain any consistency calculation. Missing entries are not failure. A score must not dominate wellbeing.

## States
No targets; no anchors logged; partial day; completed day; historical date; reminder permission unavailable.

## Acceptance
Anchor meaning is clear; target vs actual is understandable; historical edits are explicit; language is neutral and non-punitive.
