# Screen Specification — Health

## Goal
Record and review personal wellness and medication logs with clear data provenance.
## Primary action
Complete a brief check-in or open a tracking area.

## Sections
Self-reported mood/energy/stress; sleep; medication schedule and intake logs; hydration; connected health data and sync state.

## Safety and privacy
Identify source and timestamp for connected measurements. Distinguish self-entered from Health Connect. Missing, permission-denied, stale and zero values differ. Do not diagnose, recommend dose changes or infer medical meaning from one data point. Keep sensitive notifications private by default. Explain permissions, export, deletion and disconnect.

## Acceptance
Common entries are quick. Source/timestamp available. No-data is not zero. Charts include units/range/accessibility summary. Medication UI remains factual and non-prescriptive.
