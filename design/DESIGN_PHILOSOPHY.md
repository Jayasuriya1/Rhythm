# Design Philosophy — Calm Editorial Wellness

Rhythm should feel calm, warm, clear, grounded, trustworthy and quietly encouraging. Combine editorial typography and generous spacing with familiar native Android interaction patterns and restrained wellness-inspired color.

## Principles
1. Clarity before decoration: one obvious purpose and one dominant primary action per screen.
2. Calm hierarchy: use typography, spacing and grouping before color, borders or shadows.
3. Progressive disclosure: common actions first; advanced settings contextual.
4. Honest feedback: explain outcomes; never imply unsupported health conclusions.
5. Respectful language: “Not logged yet”, not “You failed”.
6. Native, accessible interaction: don't sacrifice accessibility for novelty.
7. Consistency with purpose: shared components behave consistently; special contexts can use purpose-built layouts.

## Inspiration, not imitation
- Llama Life: task clarity and timeboxing.
- Flocus: immersive focus atmosphere.
- StudyWithMe: a focused environment around one activity.
- Pomodor: minimal timer controls.
- Visual Timer: visual communication of time.
- CleanYourTV / 0TV: clarity through reduction.
- ArtCraft: coherent presentation and visual storytelling.

## Guardrails
Avoid card-in-card layouts, too many accent colors, excessive gradients/glow/glassmorphism, tiny low-contrast labels, arbitrary radii/spacing, emoji-only status, unlabeled charts and equal prominence for every metric. Essential meaning must never depend on color alone.

## Voice
Calm, direct, supportive and specific. No medical claims, guilt or patronizing motivation.
