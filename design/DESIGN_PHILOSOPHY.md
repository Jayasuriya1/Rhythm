# Design Philosophy — Calm Editorial Wellness

## Desired character
Rhythm should feel calm, warm in tone, grounded, precise, private and quietly encouraging. The default visual experience is **OLED Pitch Black**, not a light-first interface. It should pair deep black and layered charcoal surfaces with restrained sage/mint accents, editorial typography and generous but purposeful spacing.

## What makes the reference apps feel designed
The target is not simply dark colors or rounded cards. Strong products demonstrate:
- A recognizable art direction used consistently across the whole product.
- One clear focal point per screen.
- Strong typographic contrast and a deliberate type scale.
- Consistent alignment, spacing rhythm and content widths.
- Carefully controlled density: enough information, never everything at once.
- A small number of repeatable surface and component patterns.
- Purposeful micro-interactions and feedback.
- High-quality empty, loading, error and completion states.
- Responsive composition rather than stretching phone layouts onto tablets.

## Principles
1. **Clarity before decoration:** one obvious screen purpose and one dominant primary action.
2. **Composition before cards:** group by hierarchy and alignment; use a card only for a distinct object or action.
3. **Progressive disclosure:** daily actions first; advanced configuration contextual.
4. **Calm hierarchy:** use type, spacing and surface contrast before borders, gradients or shadows.
5. **Honest feedback:** no fake progress, placeholder metrics or unsupported health conclusions.
6. **Respectful language:** “Not logged yet”, never “You failed”.
7. **Native accessibility:** preserve TalkBack, font scaling, touch targets and system settings.
8. **Adaptive by design:** phones, tablets, landscape, split-screen and resizable windows receive intentional layouts.
9. **Consistency with purpose:** shared patterns remain consistent; focus mode and alarms can have distinct art direction.

## OLED-first visual direction
- Background: true black for the main canvas.
- Surfaces: a small, ordered charcoal/green-black elevation scale.
- Primary accent: muted mint/sage, not neon emerald.
- Secondary accents: muted lavender or warm amber only when meaningfully needed.
- Text: high-contrast warm white and readable secondary gray.
- Use gradients only when they add meaning to a hero/focus atmosphere; never as a default card fill.
- Preserve Standard Dark and Light as complete themes, not afterthoughts.

## Inspiration, not imitation
- Llama Life: task clarity and timeboxing.
- Flocus: immersive focus atmosphere and optional personalization.
- StudyWithMe: one activity at a time.
- Pomodor: minimal timer controls.
- Visual Timer: visual communication of time.
- CleanYourTV / 0TV: clarity through reduction.
- ArtCraft: coherent product presentation and visual storytelling.

For every reference, extract the design principle, not its exact layout, copy, assets or branding.

## Guardrails
Avoid card-in-card layouts, a different accent per feature, neon colors, gratuitous gradients/glow/glassmorphism, arbitrary radii, inconsistent icons, tiny labels, decorative charts and equal prominence for every metric. Do not make every screen a dashboard.

## Brand voice
Calm, direct, supportive and specific. No medical claims, guilt, streak-shaming or patronizing motivation.
