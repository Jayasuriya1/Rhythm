# Design Philosophy — Calm Editorial Wellness

## Desired character
Rhythm should feel calm, warm, grounded, precise, private and quietly optimistic. Its visual identity combines deep teal, layered charcoal, muted mint, warm champagne and quiet violet with editorial typography and purposeful spacing.

## Principles
1. **Clarity before decoration:** one obvious screen purpose and one dominant primary action.
2. **Composition before cards:** group by hierarchy and alignment; cards are for distinct objects/actions, not default wrappers.
3. **Progressive disclosure:** common daily actions first; advanced configuration contextual.
4. **Calm hierarchy:** use type, spacing and tonal surface contrast before borders, gradients or shadows.
5. **Honest feedback:** no fake progress, placeholder metrics or unsupported health conclusions.
6. **Respectful language:** “Not logged yet”, never “You failed”.
7. **Native accessibility:** TalkBack, font scaling, touch targets and system settings.
8. **Adaptive by design:** phones, tablets, landscape, split-screen and resizable windows receive intentional layouts.
9. **Consistency with purpose:** shared patterns stay consistent; Focus Studio and alarms can have distinct but controlled art direction.

## Theme direction
- OLED Dark is the default: true-black canvas, layered dark teal/charcoal surfaces.
- Standard Dark is deep teal-charcoal, not a duplicate OLED mode.
- Light is warm off-white, low-glare and equally polished.
- System follows Android appearance.
- Muted mint is the primary accent; champagne and violet are occasional secondary accents.
- Avoid neon, gratuitous gradients/glow/glassmorphism and decorative motion.

## Inspiration, not imitation
Extract principles from Llama Life (task clarity), Flocus (focus atmosphere), StudyWithMe (one activity at a time), Pomodor (minimal timer), Visual Timer (glanceable time), CleanYourTV / 0TV (reduction), and ArtCraft (coherent presentation). Never copy exact layouts, copy, assets or branding.

## Guardrails
Avoid card-in-card layouts, a different accent per feature, arbitrary radii, inconsistent icons, tiny labels, decorative charts and equal prominence for every metric. Do not make every screen a dashboard.

## Brand voice
Calm, direct, supportive and specific. No medical claims, guilt, streak-shaming or patronizing motivation.
